## Unused Sprites

Version found: **2.1.0**
Status: **Unused** (as far as I know)

### Description

Two **unused Lucy sprites** were found in the files: a **variant** of Lucy’s normal sprite that has her **smiling** in her **poses**, and a **drunk** variant which does the same thing more or less with **Lucy** drunk. 

The sprites have their **own** animation **data**, with the second sprite having a separate **.json** file.

### Future Use

These sprites have been confirmed to be used in the **Week 2.5 Update**, specifically where in the song **Shimmer**, there’s a 50/50 **chance** you get one of these sprites, with Lucy no longer having a stern expression in this song.
