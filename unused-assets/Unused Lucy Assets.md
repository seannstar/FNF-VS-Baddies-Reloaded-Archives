Version found: **2.1.0**
Status: **Unused** (as far as I know)

## Description

Two **unused Lucy sprites** were found in the files: a **variant** of Lucy’s normal sprite that has her **smiling** in her **poses**, and a **drunk** variant which does the same thing more or less with **Lucy** drunk.
